NEW FACES

Mr Pilkington
Owner of Foxwood: a large, neglected, old-fashioned farm. An easy-going gentleman farmer who spends most of his time fishing or hunting. (Look for: hunting cap.)
Mr Frederick
Owner of Pinchfield: smaller and better kept. A tough, shrewd man, always in lawsuits, known for driving hard bargains. (Look for: bowler hat.)
NoteFoxwood and Pinchfield are the two farms next to Animal Farm. Their owners dislike each other so much that they can hardly agree on anything, even to protect their own interests.

2 · THE FRIGHTENED NEIGHBOURS

Pilkington and Frederick are on permanently bad terms. Still, both are thoroughly frightened by the rebellion and desperate to stop their own animals learning about it.
At first they laugh at the idea of animals running a farm.
the farmersIt'll all be over in a fortnight! The animals on the Manor Farm are always fighting, and they're starving to death!

When the animals clearly don't starve, the story changes:

What's happeningThe neighbouring farmers are scared that their own animals will rebel too, so they spread lies, first that Animal Farm is failing and then that it's full of horrors. Notice they won't even use the new name.

What's happeningThis page shows two very different attitudes. Boxer is horrified that he may have killed someone, even a human, and Snowball says "war is war". Mollie, meanwhile, hid. The stable-lad turns out to be alive, but Boxer didn't know that when he was crying.

📖 UNDERSTANDING CHAPTER 4

The chapter in 6 steps

  1. Pigeons spread the story and the song to other farms. Jones complains at the pub, and the other farmers don't really help.
  2. Pilkington (Foxwood) and Frederick (Pinchfield) hate each other, but both fear the rebellion and spread lies about Animal Farm.
  3. Beasts of England spreads everywhere, and animals on other farms start to rebel.
  4. In October, Jones and his men attack. Snowball, who has studied Julius Caesar, leads the defence with a fake retreat and an ambush.
  5. Snowball is wounded but knocks Jones down; Boxer strikes a stable-lad. The men flee. One sheep dies.
  6. Snowball and Boxer get "Animal Hero, First Class". It's named the Battle of the Cowshed, and the gun becomes a ceremonial cannon.

What the book says vs. interpretation

BOOK SAYS Snowball planned and led the defence, charged Jones and was wounded. Boxer and Snowball received the medals. Mollie hid. The book's account of the battle does not mention Napoleon at all.

INTERPRETATION The humans' lies (that the animals are starving and eat each other) show how people in power describe a rebellion they fear. And the spread of Beasts of England shows that an idea can travel even when it's punished.

WORTH NOTICING Boxer and Snowball react very differently to violence: one cries, and the other says "War is war". Also remember exactly who did what in this battle.
